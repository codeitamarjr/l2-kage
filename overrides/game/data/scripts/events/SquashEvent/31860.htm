Puss the Cat:
Happy Halloween, hero! I work for Gardener Fordson of Floran, and this year his magical pumpkin seeds have grown a little strange. Help me tend them and I'll make it worth your while.
Monsters all over Aden carry Nectar. Bring it to me and I will trade you seeds or a chrono instrument.
Double-click a seed to plant it, then target the young pumpkin and use Nectar on it until it grows. Be quick: pumpkins don't last long. Large pumpkins can only be broken with a chrono instrument. Good luck!
